Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy applies to Dialogue credit packs purchased from Random Character Generator.

Unused packs

You can request a full refund for any completely unused pack. There is no limit on the number of unused packs you can refund.

Partially used packs

Each account can receive a full-price refund for one partially used pack. This is a one-time allowance per account.

Further partially used packs and fully used packs are not covered by this voluntary refund policy. Your statutory rights still apply.

Request a refund

For purchase enquiries, use our contact page.

What happens to your credits

We remove the remaining credits associated with refunded packs. We may pause purchases and credit spending while we reconcile a refund.

Your rights

This policy does not limit your mandatory consumer rights.
