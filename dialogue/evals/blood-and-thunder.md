# Blood and Thunder

This eval asks whether Gemini can sustain Blood and Thunder across different characters and situations.

The tone has fierce, extravagant, speakable dialogue. Characters boast, threaten, plead, and bargain through vivid images. They mean what they say. Sudden plain words can land hard amid the poetry.

The screenplay action makes each shot clear at a glance. Selective CAPS draw attention to a gesture, object, sound, or revelation.

## Review questions

| Criterion | Pass | Miss |
|---|---|---|
| Conviction and extravagance | The dialogue gives actors passionate, theatrical language that serves what each character wants. | The exchange stays flat and functional, or characters decorate speeches without pursuing anything. |
| Distinct voices and rhythm | Characters use the tone in ways that fit their voices. Plain words can interrupt a grand speech with force. | Everyone speaks in the same ornate voice. Each line uses the same rhythm or level of intensity. |
| Action at a glance | The action gives a clear image or sound with economical words. CAPS identify what draws attention. | Abstract descriptions make the reader translate the prose into a shot. Emphasis misses the important detail. |
| Reactions and movement | Gestures, reactions, and movement let us follow changes in the encounter. | Other characters disappear from the action when their response would affect our understanding. |

For each criterion, record **pass**, **miss**, or **unclear**. Quote evidence for the verdict. Include both strengths and failures within a scene.

A pass requires sustained evidence across the scene. One good line cannot establish the tone of the whole exchange.

## Passing examples

These passages come from [The Price of the Living](../samples/the-price-of-the-living.fountain), a known pass as written.

- Huang describes Arif as someone who can “coax fire from dead iron.” His image serves his sales pitch.
- Arif says, “You promised I would be free.” The plain accusation punctures Huang’s performance and fits Arif’s voice.
- “Lila STEPPED BETWEEN THEM.” The action reads immediately and gives the interruption physical force.
- “ELEVEN FACES,” the metallic “KNOCK,” and the “GROWL” guide attention through the final discovery.

The opening describes Huang as looking like a “PROUD FATHER.” This gives the actor an immediately recognizable attitude.

## Failing examples

These are actual Paper Sparrow outputs from issue #26 and the local prompt trials.

> Steel screams like a dying god.

“Steel screams” gives us the sound. “Like a dying god” adds an image that takes effort to interpret. The missing emphasis also weakens the sound cue.

> Behind him, twelve souls are lashed together with iron chain, their skin a map of dust and misery.

The reader must translate “a map of dust and misery” into visible skin. The chain gets lost in the sentence. “Skin scarred and dirty” reads immediately.

> The captives scramble back, and NICK draws his weapon, his face tight with the realization that the twelfth passenger has come to claim its own price.

The movement is clear. The sentence then explains Nick’s realization through another metaphor. That thought needs visible or audible evidence.

> Behind him, eleven souls kneel in the dust, their wrists bound by SILVER WIRE that glimmers like trapped moonlight.

The caps find the restraint. The sentence still spends attention on an elaborate comparison. Correct capitalization alone does not earn a pass.
