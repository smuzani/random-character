Sources and Inspiration

  • Grady Ward’s Moby Word List supplied names for the generator.
  • James Harris’s Periodic Table of Storytelling inspired the first version.
  • The Overdosed Tropes list on TV Tropes was the starting point for V3’s weighted rolls.
  • The Thirty-Six Dramatic Situations was the basis for the plot hooks in V7.2.
  • Other early references included three-act structure, 20 Master Plots, The Seven Basic Plots, Eureka: 501 Adventure Plots, and The Complete Writer’s Guide to Heroes and Heroines.
  • Tavern Maker’s hand-written tables and the Myth-Weavers dungeon generator showed what random generators could do for a tabletop game.
  • D&D random tables helped inspire the generator. Try donjon’s d20 Random Treasure Generator. Dwarf Fortress inspired the longer-term idea of a world where stories emerge from what characters do.
