Privacy Policy

This is our privacy policy. It's pretty simple because we don't collect much data.

What We Collect

We use Google Analytics to see how people use the site. This tells us things like:

  • Which pages people visit
  • How long they stay
  • What country they're from (approximately)
  • What kind of device they're using (phone, computer, etc.)

None of this is tied to you personally - we can't tell who you are, and we don't want to. It's all anonymous data that helps us understand if the site is working well.

About Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies (small files stored on your device) to work. We don't set any cookies ourselves. You can block these through your browser settings if you want - some browsers even block them by default. You can also opt out of Google Analytics entirely: Google Analytics Opt-out. But we recommend using privacy browsers like Brave, DuckDuckGo, Firefox, and even Edge.

What We Don't Collect

We don't have user accounts or logins, so we don't collect:

  • Email addresses
  • Names

Since all our data is completely anonymous, we can't identify which data belongs to which visitor. This means we can't fulfill data deletion requests - there's no way for us to find or delete "your" data because we don't know which anonymous data points came from you.

How We Use The Data

We only use the anonymous analytics data to:

  • See if the site is working properly
  • Understand how people use the site
  • Figure out what to improve
  • If something breaks, we use it to find where it broke

Changes to This Policy

We might update this policy sometimes. When we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
