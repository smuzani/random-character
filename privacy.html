Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how Random Character Generator handles information when you use the character generator, Dialogue, and credit purchases.

Accounts and payments

We use Supabase to manage email sign-in, accounts, credit balances, and usage records. Your email address is used to send sign-in links.

We keep purchase references, purchased quantities, credit grants, and payment or refund status to provide credits and resolve payment issues.

Paddle handles payment details as our merchant of record. We do not receive your full card number. See Paddle’s privacy policy.

Dialogue inputs

When you generate a scene, your scene setup and character details are sent through our backend to Google’s Gemini service to generate dialogue. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

Our application logs record operational metadata such as request status, model usage, and timing. They do not intentionally include your scene text.

Browser storage and analytics

The site uses browser storage for features such as saved characters, sign-in sessions, guest sessions, and pending payment references.

Some pages include Google Analytics to measure site usage. Analytics services may use cookies and process device, browser, network, and page-visit information.

You can manage cookies and local storage in your browser. Clearing storage may remove saved characters or sign you out.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the site, Supabase provides backend services, Google processes Dialogue generation requests, and Paddle processes purchases. These providers may process technical data needed to operate their services.

Retention and requests

We retain account, usage, and payment records as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations.

Use our contact page to request access, correction, or deletion of your account information. Some payment records may need to be retained.

Changes

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes.
